Privacy Policy
Morfgage · Effective date: July 2026 · Pre-launch version
Pre-launch · Early access
Plain English summary: Morfgage collects your name, mobile number, email, and home loan details to help match you with the right lender and DSA partner. We store this data securely, never sell it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. This platform is in pre-launch — your submission is for waitlist and research purposes only.
1. Who we are

Morfgage is a home loan intelligence platform currently in pre-launch and early access phase. The platform is operated by the Morfgage founding team, based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. The company is in the process of formal incorporation. Contact us at: morfgage@gmail.com

References to "Morfgage", "we", "us", or "our" in this policy refer to the Morfgage founding team and, upon incorporation, the registered entity.

2. What data we collect and why

When you use the Morfgage platform, we may collect:

  • Contact details — name, mobile number, email address. Used to follow up on your loan enquiry and notify you when Morfgage launches in your city.
  • Location data — state and city. Used to determine whether Morfgage's DSA matching service is available in your area.
  • Financial profile data — income type, profession or employer, property PIN code, approximate loan amount, loan purpose, CIBIL score band. Used to identify lenders and DSA partners suited to your profile.
  • Usage data — pages visited, actions taken within the platform. Used to improve the platform experience. Collected only in aggregate and not linked to your identity.

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service described. We do not collect your Aadhaar number, PAN number, bank account details, or any financial documents. Documents go directly from you to your lender — Morfgage never collects, stores, or transmits your loan documents.

3. Legal basis for processing (DPDPA 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, we process your personal data on the following basis:

  • Consent — You provide explicit consent by checking the consent box on the intake form before submitting your details.
  • Legitimate interest — We may process usage data in aggregate to improve platform performance, where this does not override your privacy rights.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us at morfgage@gmail.com. Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

4. How we store your data

During the pre-launch phase, data submitted through this platform is stored in:

  • Your browser's local storage — on your own device, accessible only to you on that device.
  • A private Google Sheet — accessible only to the Morfgage founding team. This sheet is not publicly accessible and is protected by Google Workspace security.

Upon incorporation, data will be migrated to a secure, India-based cloud infrastructure with appropriate technical and organisational safeguards.

We retain your data for a maximum of 24 months from the date of submission, after which it is deleted. You may request earlier deletion at any time.

5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

We may share your data with:

  • DSA partners — If you proceed to the DSA matching step, your contact details and loan profile may be shared with a Morfgage partner DSA for the purpose of following up on your loan enquiry. You will be informed before this sharing takes place.
  • Google LLC — As the provider of Google Sheets and Google Apps Script, which we use for data storage during the pre-launch phase. Google's privacy policy applies to this processing.
  • Legal authorities — If required by law, court order, or regulatory authority.

We do not share your data with lenders directly. Data shared with DSA partners is subject to their own privacy practices and any agreements they have with you.

6. Your rights under DPDPA 2023

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access — You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correction — You may request that inaccurate or incomplete data be corrected.
  • Right to erasure — You may request that your data be deleted. We will comply within 30 days.
  • Right to withdraw consent — You may withdraw consent for processing at any time.
  • Right to grievance redressal — You may lodge a complaint about our data practices.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at morfgage@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Request". We will respond within 30 days.

7. Pre-launch and early access disclaimer

Morfgage is currently in pre-launch. The platform is operational for demonstration and early-access research purposes only. Submitting your details does not guarantee:

  • A response from the Morfgage team
  • A loan enquiry being processed or matched
  • Connection to a DSA partner
  • Any specific loan outcome or rate

Morfgage does not lend money, does not guarantee loan approval, and does not act as a financial advisor. Interest rates shown on the platform are indicative and subject to change based on lender policies and your individual credit assessment.

8. Cookies and local storage

This platform uses browser local storage (not cookies) to remember your session and store your profile data on your own device. This data does not leave your device unless you submit the intake form. You may clear your browser's local storage at any time through your browser settings.

We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy as the platform evolves or as legal requirements change. Material changes will be communicated by updating the effective date at the top of this page. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.

10. Contact and grievance officer

For any privacy-related queries, data requests, or complaints:

  • Email: morfgage@gmail.com
  • Subject line: Privacy / Data Request
  • Response time: Within 30 working days
  • Location: Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

Upon incorporation, a formal Grievance Officer will be designated as required under the DPDPA 2023 and the Information Technology Act 2000.
