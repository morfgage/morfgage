Questions

Frequently asked

Is Morfgage free for borrowers?+
Yes — completely free for borrowers to use. There is no charge to view rates, compare lenders, or join the waitlist.
How is Morfgage different from Paisabazaar?+

Paisabazaar is a broad financial marketplace — personal loans, credit cards, business loans, home loans. Home loans are one tab among many. Morfgage does only home loans, LAP, and balance transfers. That specialisation means every feature we build, every lender we research, and every piece of guidance we offer is focused specifically on the home loan journey — not divided across a dozen product types.

The deeper difference is in how we connect you. Paisabazaar shows you lender options and lets you apply. Morfgage matches you to a DSA who is already empanelled with the lender that fits your specific profile — income type, CIBIL score, property location, loan amount. That DSA knows that lender's internal criteria because they work with them daily. Files go to the right door the first time, reducing the risk of a rejection on your credit record.

Morfgage is built by people with experience in how home loan decisions are actually made — not just by reading lender websites. That context informs how we guide borrowers, not just an algorithm.

I don't know my CIBIL score. Can I still use Morfgage?+
Our rate guide shows minimum CIBIL requirements for each lender type, so you can see which lenders are more likely to consider lower CIBIL scores. You can check your actual score free at the official CIBIL website (cibil.com) before approaching any lender.
When will Morfgage go live in Bengaluru?+
Morfgage is currently in early access. We are building our DSA partner network and validating the platform in Bengaluru. Everyone on the waitlist will be first to know when we go live.
What types of home loans does Morfgage cover?+
Home loans for new and resale property purchase, plot and construction loans, Loan Against Property (LAP), and balance transfers from your existing lender to a lower-rate option.
I'm self-employed or have informal income. Can Morfgage help?+
Our rate guide includes lender type information — PSU banks, private banks, HFCs, and NBFCs — and different lender types have different criteria for self-employed borrowers. Use the comparison to shortlist lenders that are more likely to consider your income profile before approaching them.
